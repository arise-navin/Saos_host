## Acceptance rules

- *Materially unhealthy* means below **50**. That is the product's existing "Needs work" band, and it is an assumption for you to review.
- Dilution tolerance: 5 points. Stability tolerance: 5 points for a ±10% change in any number. Masking slack: 1 band.
- P1 Monotonicity: fixing a defect never lowers a score, and adding one never raises it.
- P2 Severity ordering: Systemic costs more than Critical, which costs more than High, then Moderate, then Low.
- P3 No masking: one materially unhealthy module shows in the Overall band **and** number.
- P4 Dilution resistance: a clean tail growing from 10 to 10,000 records moves a defective core's dimension by at most the tolerance.
- P5 Authoring invariance: one condition restated as two rules (same family, or same charge key) scores the same.
- P6 Coverage honesty: an unevaluated rule never scores better than the same rule passing.
- P7 Applicability neutrality: an excluded dimension scores the same as an absent one.
- P8 Stability: see the tolerance above.
